Privacy Policy

CODE:Y RECORDS
Last updated: June 2, 2026

1. Overview

CODE:Y RECORDS ("we", "us") operates automated music promotion tools that post content to social media platforms including TikTok, YouTube, and Threads. This privacy policy explains how we handle data in connection with these tools.

2. Data We Collect

Our tools do not collect, store, or process any personal data from third-party users. The tools operate exclusively on the account owner's behalf to publish pre-generated music promotion content.

3. How We Use Data

  • Authentication credentials are used solely to connect with social media platform APIs for content posting.
  • Post history data (timestamps, post IDs) is stored to prevent duplicate postings.
  • No user tracking, analytics collection, or data sharing with third parties occurs.

4. Data Storage

Credentials are stored as encrypted secrets in GitHub Actions. Post history is stored in the application's private GitHub repository.

5. Third-Party Services

Our tools interact with TikTok, YouTube, Threads (Meta), Cloudflare R2, and Anthropic Claude APIs. Each service's own privacy policy governs data processed by that service.

6. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us via our official channels.
